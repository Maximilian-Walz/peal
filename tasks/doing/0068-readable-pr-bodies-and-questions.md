# 0068 — Pull request bodies and questions that invite reading

## Intent

What Peal shows the human is long: `/peal:close` writes pull request bodies of a page or
more, and the planner asks questions whose whole context sits in the question's text. The
human skims them or merges without reading, which defeats the point of both. When this
is done, a pull request body opens with a short summary written for the person who
merges, and each question is short, with a label, and with its shared context said once
before the questions. This also matches what Belfry shows (Belfry #188 condenses a merge
item from the body's sections, #189 titles a question with its `header` and puts the
session's preceding message above the questions), without Peal depending on Belfry.

## Scope

- The pull request body that `/peal:close` writes (its sections, generated by the close
  scripts and the command's prompt): first `## Summary`, at most five bullets on what
  changes as the human will notice it, not how it was built; then `## Decisions` and
  `## Human steps` when there are any; the task's longer text after them. The session's
  final message after close is one or two sentences with the PR's link, not a retelling.
- The planner and `/peal:work` asking the human: every `AskUserQuestion` has a short
  `header` (a label of up to 12 characters) and a question of one or two sentences; the
  context the questions share (the plan's summary, what the planner found) is the
  session's message right before asking, not repeated in each question.
- The prompts of the other commands that ask (`/peal:split`, `/peal:revise`, `/peal:retire`,
  `/peal:milestone-review`, `/peal:setup`) follow the same rule.

## Done when

- The close harness asserts the body's section order (banners, `## Summary`, `## Decisions`,
  `## Human steps`, task lines, pr.sections, `## Outcome`, ideas, commits); the prompt
  harness asserts that close.md asks for at most five one-line Summary bullets (a
  recommendation, not a script refusal).
- The prompt harness covers the question rule in the planner's and `/peal:work`'s prompts.
- `docs/design.md` states both conventions where it describes close and the planner.
